Unplugged is one of two FIRST Tech Challenge teams representing Timișoara's German-language high school, Liceul Teoretic "Nikolaus Lenau." Each season we take on a new international game, then spend the year designing, machining, wiring, and coding a robot to compete in it — alongside writing the engineering notebook that documents every decision along the way.

We compete in the West Region of FTC Romania, sharing league meets and scrimmages with teams from across Timișoara, and we take part in outreach work that brings robotics and STEM to younger students across the county.

Where we've been

2020

Team founded

Unplugged is established at Lenau and registered as Team #19257 in FIRST Tech Challenge Romania.

2024

National stage

The team qualifies for the national stage of the BRD FIRST Tech Challenge Championship.

2025

County-wide outreach

Members mentor younger students as part of "STEAM la puterea inEDU," a program that reached over 3,400 children across Timiș county.

Now

Season in progress

Regular league meets against Timișoara teams including RoboDojo, CyberMoon, and AtlasCNB, building toward this year's regional qualifier.

How the team is organized

BUILD

Mechanical & Design

CAD, fabrication, and iteration on the physical robot — from first prototype to competition-ready chassis.

CODE

Programming

Writing the control and autonomous software that turns a wired-up robot into one that scores points.

DOCS

Strategy & Notebook

Match strategy, scouting, and the engineering notebook that records the reasoning behind the robot.

OUTREACH

Community

Workshops and mentoring that bring robotics to younger students in Timișoara and Timiș county.

Joining Unplugged

New members join at the start of each school year. No prior robotics experience is required — just curiosity, and a willingness to learn CAD, code, or how to write up a build clearly, whichever side of the team fits you best.
